# Problem 10 — Style the website

## Design changes

- **More blue and white:** a light blue-and-white dotted page background. A Yale blue nav bar and footer with a white-and-blue stripe. A Yale blue hero with white diagonal stripes and a white frame. White product cards with a Yale blue top edge, and white feature boxes with a Yale blue side bar.
- **Papyrus font** for the main page titles (every page's heading, e.g. "Wear your Bulldog spirit.", "Products", each product's name) and the Campus Customs logo text.
- **Bulldog chatbot avatar (Handsome Dan):** a cute cartoon bulldog in a Yale blue collar replaces the chat button and sits in the chat header. Whenever a user starts a new chat (opening a fresh chat, or pressing Start over), he jumps in with a bounce and says "Woof! Let's find your gear!"
- **The bulldog's mood:** he's happy (smiling, panting, wiggling) while the user is chatting or clicking product cards. After 30 seconds with neither, he gets **sad** (droopy ears, frown, falling tear, "getting lonely…"). After 60 seconds he gets **stressed** (wide eyes, gritted teeth, sweat drops, shaking). A note next to the closed chat button says "Handsome Dan misses you…" or "Handsome Dan is stressed! Chat with him?". Sending a chat message or clicking a product card makes him happy again.
- **Spiders** crawl around the screen, wandering, turning, and stopping now and then. They don't block clicks.
- **Product cards pop out when clicked:** the card grows and lifts off the page, then the product page opens.
- **Gon and Naruto** stand in the bottom-left corner and greet users, because starting a college education is like starting a shonen arc. They take turns giving encouraging lines in a speech bubble, and the lines change with each page (Home, Products, a product page, About, Log in / Create account). One line always shows how many points the user needs for their next reward. Users can hide them.
- **Dynamic point tracker** in the nav bar: +10 points for interacting with a product card, +50 for buying. It shows the score and a progress bar to the goal, with a floating "+10" or "+50" each time. The first goal is 300 points. Each time a goal is met, the user gets a reward (10% off at Campus Customs vending machines, with a code) and the goal moves up by 100 (300 → 400 → 500…). Points and rewards are saved in the browser, separately for each account.

## Why

I made the site more interactive and energetic by adding characters. It should make younger customers laugh and want to engage more with the site. Also, the Yale coloring and papyrus font will sell the image of Yale as a respected institution, while the bulldog chatbot avatar will motivate people to not only use the chatbot but return to the site to see the dog since they are worried about it. Finally, the dynamic point system gamifies the site to make users want to interact and shop to accumulate points, for the reward or even for the intrinsic dopamine boost.
